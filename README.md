This calculator was created in response to the absence of Mims' Rucoy Calculator discord bot as well as the previous inaccuracies of the current most popular rucoy calculator app available on the playstore at the time of its creation. The aim of this calculator is to provide highly accurate and competent results through a simple and easy to navigate interface while remaining highly accessible to anyone wanting to use it.

The interface of this calculator is simple by design. To use, simply input your base level, skill level, extra equips, and your current weapon's attack. The results will indicate what you are currently able to train as well as how many stats are needed to progress to the next mob. The calculator allows for the selection either training type (whether power training or AFK training), so ensure to specify one. There are also advanced options available that allow you to tweak two elements: target percent efficiency, and whether or not you would like to enable crit ring. Target percent efficiency describes the desired efficiency when training any mob. By default, it is set to 90% which in turn directs the calculator to find the longest lasting mob to train at a tick rate of at least 3,240 ticks/hour. Enabling crit ring will alter the way critical hits are calculated. Enabling crit ring will also likely require you to account for a ring swapout in the "Extra Equips" entry.
